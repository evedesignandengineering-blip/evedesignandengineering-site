LVDesign Web

Low-voltage network design in the browser. Open DFIS .net and LV Design .lvd files, scale to the DSTR peak, check volt drop against 12 V (overhead) and 14.4 V (underground), merge transformers and view the network on aerial or property maps.

Open LVDesign Web →

Drawing & Quote Checker

Compare electrical design drawing CU items, materials and quantities against a quote PDF. Flag missing items and discrepancies, then export a comparison report.

Open Drawing & Quote Checker →
✎

Quote from Drawing

Upload a Western Power design drawing PDF and generate a DQM quote file of CU IDs and quantities. Optionally upload an existing quote to compare it with the drawing. Choose the Gemini or Claude AI engine.

Open Quote from Drawing →

kVA ↔ Amps Calculator

Instantly convert kVA to amps or amps to kVA. Select 1-phase at 240 V or 3-phase at 415 V; the result updates as you type.

Open kVA ↔ Amps Calculator →

CAD Quantity Takeoff

Analyse supported CAD drawings to calculate layer length totals and cell or block counts. Review non-zero quantities and export results to Excel or CSV.

Open Quantity Takeoff →

GIS Image Mosaic Tool

Combine georeferenced GIS aerial-image ZIP exports into one CAD-ready GeoTIFF. Supports world files and projection metadata, with output reprojection for GDA94 / MGA Zone 50.

Open GIS Image Mosaic Tool →

Distribution GIS & SOW Designer

Place distribution assets, draw cable routes, group equipment and draft copyable Scope of Works. Includes a shared asset library managed by the EVE admin and links to Western Power design catalogues.
