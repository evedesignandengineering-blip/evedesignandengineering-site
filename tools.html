Check and build quotes

Three ways to work with a design drawing PDF and its DQM quote. Use the rule-based checker for a quick, repeatable comparison, or the AI tools for drawings that need reading in more detail.

PDF vs Quote Checker

Compare a design drawing PDF with its quote PDF using fixed rules. Parses catalogue codes and quantities, flags missing or mismatched items and checks project value and depot distance. Works with digitally generated PDFs.

AI CU Comparison Checker

AI reads the drawing’s CU callouts and compares them with the DQM quote, including AUX, travel, cable and polypipe, and directional drilling checks. Export a comparison report.

Quote from Drawing

Generate a DQM quote file of CU IDs and quantities from a Western Power design drawing PDF. Add depot, round-trip and switching details, and optionally check the result against an existing quote.
