How the checker treats differences

Drawing vs quote: the drawing is treated as the design baseline. Quote-only items are ignored by default. Drawing items missing from the quote, quantity differences, and material/description conflicts are flagged. Additional quote rules: AUX_013 must be 1 unit up to $50,000 project value, 3 units from $50,001 to $200,000, and 12 units from $200,001 upward. For AUX_005, the checker first subtracts the AUX_005 amount already included in total internal cost (quoted AUX_005 units × $174.13), then calculates the expected quantity as 4 units per $10,000 band or part thereof of the adjusted internal cost. It checks the expected quantity and AUX_005 total amount against the DQM quote. Cable-to-polypipe rules: 1/16/25 mm cable requires 63 mm polypipe; 120/240 mm cable requires 110 mm polypipe; 400HV cable requires 160 mm polypipe. Directional-drill polypipe installation quantity must equal haul quantity for each matching pipe size. Each CN54x unit requires 2 Cable_07x units. Incorrect sizes or quantities are highlighted as high-priority discrepancies; unclear values are flagged for manual review.

Important: this first browser-based version extracts selectable PDF text and uses CU-pattern recognition plus text similarity. It is not a hosted AI model and cannot reliably interpret scanned/raster drawings or visually infer symbols. Always verify flagged items against the original drawing before approving a quote.
