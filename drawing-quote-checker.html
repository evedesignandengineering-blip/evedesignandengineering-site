How the checker treats differences

Drawing vs quote: the drawing is treated as the design baseline. Quote-only items are ignored by default, as requested. Drawing items missing from the quote, quantity differences, and material/description conflicts are flagged. Similar CU labels and descriptions are matched conservatively, and uncertain matches are marked for review.

Important: this first browser-based version extracts selectable PDF text and uses CU-pattern recognition plus text similarity. It is not a hosted AI model and cannot reliably interpret scanned/raster drawings or visually infer symbols. Always verify flagged items against the original drawing before approving a quote.
