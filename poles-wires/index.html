Ground profile alignment

    Click a pole on the map, then Add to profile. Pole length starts as the existing length from GIS or inspections; type a new length for a proposed pole. Setting depth = 10% of length + 0.6 m, height above ground = length − setting depth.

    Circuit segments (optional)

    Only needed if you want the Segments sheet of the workbook filled (manual §10.1.3); your current workbooks leave it empty. Use PNW conductor codes (for example LI, 7/14CU, IOD). Nothing is filled in for you.
