How the checker treats differences

CU comparison and supplementary checks: Drawing quantities are summed only from explicit drawing CU: callouts and compared with the DQM quote’s Compatible Units table. Repeated entries are totalled; NON CU: and DCSH: notes are excluded; quote-only CUs are informational extras. The checker also validates AUX_013 against project value (1, 3 or 12 by value band), AUX_005 against adjusted total internal cost, each drawing CN54x against two CABLE_07 units, cable-to-polypipe sizing (1/16/25 mm → 63 mm, 120/240 mm → 110 mm, 400 HV → 160 mm), DDrill installation versus haul length by diameter, and depot travel against road distance. If value, pipe-size or route evidence is missing, it flags the uncertainty rather than inventing a result.

Important: this the checker sends extracted PDF text and selected page images to an AI model for comparison. AI can misread scanned drawings, symbols, quantities or table alignment. Always verify flagged and matched items against the original drawing and quote before approving a quote.
